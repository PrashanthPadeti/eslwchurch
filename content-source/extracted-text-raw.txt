El Shaddai Living Waters Church 
Sydney – Australia 
Welcome to El Shaddai Living Waters Church Sydney 
A Christ Centred, Spirit Filled Church 
Welcome to El Shaddai Living Waters Church Sydney. 
We are a church family where people can come as they are, encounter Jesus, grow in His 
Word, experience the presence and power of the Holy Spirit, and become a blessing to 
others. 
Our name reflects who we believe God is and what we desire to see in His Church 
We warmly welcome you to El Shaddai Living Waters Church Sydney. 
May the living waters of the Holy Spirit bring hope, healing, restoration and new life 
to you and your family. 
God bless you. 
Church Name: 
El Shaddai means God Almighty. 
God is our source, our strength, our provider and our refuge. 
Genesis 17:1  I am God Almighty; walk before me faithfully. 
We chose El Shaddai because we want to remember that this church belongs to God 
and everything we do must depend on Him. 
Living Waters 
Jesus said in John 7:38:  Whoever believes in me... rivers of living water will flow from 
within them. 
Living Waters represents the life giving work of the Holy Spirit.  
Our desire is to see those living waters flow into families, our community and beyond. 
We desire this church to be a place of: 
 
Prayer  
 
Worship  
 
The Word of God

Ezekiel 47:9  Everything will live where the river flows. 
Our prayer:  Let the Living Waters flow through this church and bring life to

Our Vision 
Our Vision: To make Jesus Christ known through the Word of God and the 
power of the Holy Spirit, and to see lives, families and communities transformed 
for the glory of God. 
Our vision is not only to gather people on Sunday. 
We want to see: 
 
People come to know Jesus  
 
Lives transformed  
 
Families strengthened  
 
Believers grow spiritually  
 
The Holy Spirit move  
 
The broken restored  
 
People receive prayer and encouragement  
 
New disciples raised  
 
The Gospel taken beyond the church  
Vision Bible Verse 
Matthew 28:19–20  Go and make disciples of all nations... teaching them to obey 
everything I have commanded you. 
Our vision is therefore not only to have a church, but to make disciples who will 
make Jesus known to others. 
Our Mission 
Our Mission: Our mission is to preach Jesus Christ, reach the lost, lead people to 
salvation, baptise and disciple believers, build a strong prayer and worship life, 
minister in the power and gifts of the Holy Spirit, care for families, reach our 
community through outreach, and prepare believers to live faithfully and be 
ready for the return of Jesus Christ.

Our mission will be carried out through:   
Worship: To glorify Jesus Christ through worship and praise. 
Word: To faithfully teach and preach the Bible. 
Prayer: To build a church that depends on prayer. 
Holy Spirit: To welcome and follow the work of the Holy Spirit. 
Discipleship: To help believers grow from spiritual infancy into maturity. 
Pastoral Care : To encourage, guide and support individuals and families. 
Our vision is not simply to build a church, but to build people for Christ - to win souls, 
make disciples, walk in the power of the Holy Spirit, and prepare the Bride of Christ for 
the coming of our Lord Jesus. 
Our Heart fo outreach 
To take the Gospel beyond the church and reach people who may not know Jesus. 
We want to reach our neighbours, workplaces, families, friends and the wider 
community with the love and message of Jesus Christ. 
Mission Bible Verse  Mark 16:15 Go into all the world and preach the Gospel to all 
creation. 
And Acts 1:8 reminds us:  You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; 
and you will be my witnesses... 
Our mission is not only to receive from God. It is also to go and share what God 
has given us.  
To Those Who Become Members 
If you decide to become part of El Shaddai Living Waters Church, you are not simply 
becoming someone who attends Sunday services. 
You become part of the vision and mission of the church. 
The vision becomes our vision. 
The mission becomes our mission. 
 We pray together. 
 We worship together. 
 We serve together.

 We grow together.

 We carry one another together. 
Church is not a place where one person does everything. 
1 Corinthians 12:27  Now you are the body of Christ, and each one of you is a part of 
it. 
 Every person has a place. 
 Every person has a gift. 
 Every person has a responsibility. 
Pastor and Members - We Are Accountable to One Another 
I also want to make something very clear as Pastor. 
I am accountable to God first, and I am also accountable to the church to 
faithfully serve, teach and shepherd according to the Word of God. 
And as members, you also have a responsibility to walk according to the Word of God 
and support the vision and mission of the church. 
This is not one sided. 
Pastor and members need one another. 
Hebrews 13:17  Have confidence in your leaders and submit to their authority, because 
they keep watch over you as those who must give an account. 
This verse reminds me that I have to give an account for how I shepherd Gods 
people. 
And 1 Peter 5:2–3 says:  Be shepherds of Gods flock that is under your care... not 
lording it over those entrusted to you, but being examples to the flock. 
So as Pastor: 
I will serve you. 
I will pray for you. 
I will teach you the Word. 
I will encourage you. 
I will lead by example. 
And I ask you: 
Pray for me. 
Pray for my family.

A Note from the Pastor 
Thank you for taking the time to learn more about El Shaddai Living Waters Church 
Sydney Australia  
It is our prayer that this will be a place where you can know Jesus, grow in His Word, 
experience the presence of the Holy Spirit, and find a church family where you can serve 
and grow. 
If you are not currently attending a church, or if you are prayerfully looking for a church 
family where you can be part of a Christ centred and Spirit filled vision, we warmly invite 
you to come and worship with us. 
If our vision and mission speak to your heart, we would be blessed to have you become 
part of what God is doing through El Shaddai Living Waters Church Sydney. 
We are not looking to simply fill a church building. We want to build people for Christ, 
reach souls, make disciples, serve our community and prepare one another for 
the coming of Jesus. 
Thank you for visiting us and for taking the time to read about our church. May the Lord 
guide you, bless you and your family, and lead you into all that He has prepared for you. 
With prayers and every blessing, 
Pastor Daniel 
Founder and Senior Pastor 
El Shaddai Living Waters Church Sydney
