# Content Checklist — What I Need Before Building

Everything below is what turns an empty repo into a finished site. Nothing here
requires technical skill. Most of it already exists somewhere — in a bulletin, a
constitution document, a WhatsApp group, or a pastor's head.

**How to hand it over:** create one Google Drive folder called
`Church Website Content` with two things inside — a single Google Doc for all
text, and a subfolder called `Photos`. Share the folder and send me the link.
One doc and one folder beats fifteen separate messages.

**Don't wait for perfection.** Section A (branding), E (service times) and F
(location) are enough for me to start Phase 1. The rest can arrive as it comes.

---

## Priority order

| Tier | Sections | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Must have to start** | A, E, F | Identity, service times, location |
| **Must have to launch** | B, C, D, H | The actual substance of the site |
| **Can follow after launch** | G, I, J | Photos and translations can be added via the CMS |

---

## A. Identity and branding

- [ ] **Church logo** — SVG or AI/EPS preferred. Otherwise PNG with a
      transparent background, at least 1000px wide. If the only copy is a photo
      of a printed sign, send it anyway and say so — it can be redrawn.
- [ ] **Full legal name** of the church, exactly as registered
- [ ] **Preferred short name** for display (e.g. "El Shaddai Living Waters" vs
      the full legal name)
- [ ] **Tagline or motto**, if one exists (under 10 words)
- [ ] **Brand colours**, if already established — otherwise I'll use the palette
      in the project plan
- [ ] **Denomination or affiliation**, if any, and whether it should be shown

---

## B. Core text

Word counts are guidance, not rules. Shorter and true beats longer and padded.

- [ ] **Mission statement** — 1–2 sentences, roughly 25 words
- [ ] **Vision statement** — 1–2 sentences, roughly 25 words
- [ ] **Core values** — 3–6 items, each a short phrase plus one explanatory
      sentence
- [ ] **Our story / About** — 250–400 words. When was the church founded, by
      whom, what has changed, where is it now. Specific beats generic: a real
      date and a real first meeting place is worth more than a paragraph about
      being a welcoming community.
- [ ] **Statement of faith / What we believe** — 6–10 short points. If a
      denominational statement is used, just name it and link it.
- [ ] **What to expect on your first visit** — 150–200 words. Genuinely one of
      the most-read pages on any church site. Cover: how long the service runs,
      what people wear, whether there is childcare, where to park, whether
      someone will greet them, whether they will be asked to stand up or give.
- [ ] **A welcome line from the pastor** — 1–2 sentences for the homepage

---

## C. Leadership

For each person to appear on the site:

- [ ] Full name
- [ ] Role or title
- [ ] Short bio — **pastor: 100–150 words**, others: 50–80 words
- [ ] Photo — see specs in Section G
- [ ] Display order (who appears first)
- [ ] Contact email, only if they are happy for it to be public

Start with the senior pastor and 2–4 key leaders. A page with five real people is
better than one with fifteen placeholders.

---

## D. Ministries

For each ministry — typically Youth, Women's, Men's, Children's / Sunday School,
Worship, Prayer, Outreach:

- [ ] Ministry name
- [ ] Leader or contact person
- [ ] When and where it meets
- [ ] Description — 60–100 words: who it is for, what happens, how to join
- [ ] One representative photo
- [ ] Age range, if relevant (particularly for children's and youth ministries)

---

## E. Services and schedule

- [ ] **Regular weekly services** — for each one: day, start time, end time,
      service name, language, and venue if more than one is used

      Example format:
      | Day | Time | Service | Language | Venue |
      |---|---|---|---|---|
      | Sunday | 9:30 – 11:30 am | Morning Worship | English | Main Hall |
      | Sunday | 12:00 – 1:30 pm | Worship | (2nd language) | Main Hall |
      | Wednesday | 7:00 – 8:30 pm | Prayer Meeting | English | Prayer Room |
      | Friday | 7:00 – 9:00 pm | Youth Fellowship | English | Youth Room |

- [ ] **Anything that varies** — e.g. communion on the first Sunday, monthly
      all-night prayer, quarterly baptism services
- [ ] **A recent weekly bulletin**, so I can see the real shape of a week
- [ ] **Any upcoming events** for the next 3 months — title, date, time, venue,
      one-paragraph description
- [ ] **Annual recurring events** — anniversary, Christmas and Easter services,
      conventions, retreats, VBS

---

## F. Location and contact

- [ ] **Full street address**, exactly as it should appear
- [ ] **Google Maps link** to the exact entrance — not just the suburb
- [ ] **Parking information** — where, how much, is it free on Sundays
- [ ] **Public transport** — nearest train station or bus stop and walking time
- [ ] **Accessibility** — step-free access, accessible toilets, hearing loop
- [ ] **Phone number**, and whether it should be public
- [ ] **Enquiries email address**
- [ ] **Social media links** — Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp
- [ ] **ABN**
- [ ] **ACNC charity registration number**, if registered

---

## G. Photos and media

**This is the single biggest determinant of how the site looks.** Ten good
photographs will do more than any amount of design work. If the church has
someone with a decent camera, one hour after a Sunday service covers most of it.

### Specifications

| Use | Size | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero (homepage) | 2400 × 1350 (16:9) | JPG | Landscape, wide. Congregation, worship or the building. |
| Leadership portraits | 800 × 800 (square) | JPG | Face centred, plain-ish background, consistent style |
| Ministry cards | 1200 × 800 (3:2) | JPG | Real people doing the actual activity |
| Gallery | 1600px+ long edge | JPG | Any orientation |
| Logo | Any, vector preferred | SVG / PNG | Transparent background |

Phone photos are completely fine if taken in good light. Send originals — do not
compress or resize them first.

### Shot list

- [ ] 2–3 wide shots of the congregation during worship
- [ ] Exterior of the building, clearly showing the entrance people should use
- [ ] Interior of the main hall, empty and well lit
- [ ] Portrait of each leader
- [ ] One photo per ministry
- [ ] 10–20 assorted event photos for the gallery
- [ ] Optional: 1–2 short video clips for the homepage

### Before sending

- [ ] Confirm you hold **consent for every identifiable person**, especially
      children. If in doubt, leave the photo out. This needs to be settled before
      launch, not after.

---

## H. Giving

- [ ] **Account name**
- [ ] **BSB**
- [ ] **Account number**
- [ ] **PayID**, if set up
- [ ] **Reference format** you want people to use (e.g. `Tithe - Surname`)
- [ ] **Tax-deductibility position** — are general donations deductible, is there
      a separate DGR-endorsed building fund? This must be stated accurately.
- [ ] Any explanatory text about giving you want on the page

Confirm you are comfortable with bank details being publicly visible. Some
churches prefer to place them behind a contact form instead — either is
supportable, just tell me which.

---

## I. Sermons and archive

Only if you want a sermon archive at launch:

- [ ] YouTube channel URL
- [ ] For each sermon: title, speaker, date, scripture reference, YouTube link
- [ ] Whether past sermons should be backfilled, or only new ones from launch

Backfilling is data entry, so starting from launch and growing forward is
usually the pragmatic choice.

---

## J. Second language

- [ ] **Which language** — this is currently blocking the font and layout
      decisions
- [ ] **Who will translate**, and are they available ongoingly or one-off
- [ ] Translations of: mission, vision, About, service names, navigation labels,
      and the homepage welcome
- [ ] Whether the second language should be the **default** for some visitors, or
      always opt-in via the language switcher
- [ ] Whether scripture references should appear in both languages

If a regular translator is not available, say so — I would then recommend
translating only the navigation and key pages rather than committing to a fully
bilingual site that drifts out of date.

---

## K. Domain and accounts

Decisions and admin, not content:

- [ ] **Preferred domain name**, plus two backups in case the first is taken
- [ ] Confirm the church has an **ABN** (required for `.org.au`)
- [ ] **Who will own the domain and hosting accounts** — this should be a church
      role, not an individual, with a church-owned email and payment method
- [ ] **Two people** to receive CMS access (never just one)
- [ ] Existing website or social accounts to link from or redirect

---

## Quick start

If you only do one thing this week, do this:

1. Send the **logo** file
2. Send the **service times table** from Section E
3. Send the **address and Google Maps link**
4. Tell me **which second language**

That is enough for me to build the scaffold, the design system, the homepage and
the navigation. Everything else can be filled in as it arrives — and once the CMS
is live, you can add most of it yourselves without involving me at all.
